Life is not a game

(but I think I'm winning)

Unapologetically queer, this song expresses the love of someone that found themselves after losing themselves so many times.
This love is like a precious gem that you want to treasure and keep all to yourself;
yet, it is so beautiful and powerful that you also want to shout it to the world.
This new version of you is finally ready to be happy. It’s about finding peace after going through a rough time; it’s a warm sunray putting an end to a cold winter; it is love, at last.
It’s a piece of pure queer joy.

Often described as a chaotic Virgo, Fox Gunn is a London-based independent singer-songwriter who mixes her Catalan heritage in alternative pop melodies. Inspired by rock, electronic, and many other genres, she ditched her classical sheet music to create her own tunes. Fox’s tracks tell you stories about queer love, about break-ups and healing. They are packed with vulnerability and rawness but smoothed with deep emotions and realness. Her diary-like, bilingual lyrics let you into her world in an honest and badass way.
Whether you listen to her songs while crying your heart out at home or while painting the town red with your best friends, Fox Gunn will charm you with her warm smile and flirty lines.
